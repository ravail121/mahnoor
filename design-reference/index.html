Common Questions

It's okay to have questions

Is my visit completely confidential?

Yes. Everything discussed in your session stays strictly between you and Dr. Mahnoor. Your records and identity are never shared with anyone.

What happens in the first session?

The first session is simply a conversation — about how you've been feeling and what's been on your mind. There's no pressure, no judgment, and you share only what you're comfortable with.

Can I choose an online video session instead?

Yes. While booking, you can select either an in-person visit at Farooq Hospital DHA or a private online video session.

What are the consultation fees?

Fee details are shared with you at the time of booking confirmation, or you can ask us directly on WhatsApp.

Can I book for a family member?

Of course. Many appointments are made by parents, spouses, or siblings. Just add their details in the booking form.
