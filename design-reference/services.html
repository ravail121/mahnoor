Areas of Care

Whatever you're carrying, you don't have to carry it alone.

Dr. Mahnoor helps with a wide range of mental health concerns, for adults, young people, and families — in person or online.

Depression & Low Mood

Persistent sadness · low energy · loss of interest · sleep or appetite changes · feeling hopeless

Together we understand what's behind how you've been feeling, and build a gentle, practical path toward feeling like yourself again.

Anxiety & Panic

Constant worry · restlessness · racing thoughts · a pounding heart · sudden panic attacks

Learn what triggers anxiety, how to calm it in the moment, and how to feel more in control day to day.

Sleep Problems

Trouble falling asleep · waking through the night · early waking · feeling tired despite sleep

We look at what's disturbing your rest and find real ways to help you sleep — and wake — better.

OCD

Unwanted repetitive thoughts · checking · washing · rituals that feel impossible to stop

A supportive, structured approach to loosen the grip of obsessions and compulsions over time.

Stress & Burnout

Feeling overwhelmed · exhausted · irritable · unable to switch off from work or life pressures

Space to breathe, understand what's draining you, and build healthier ways to cope and recover.

Mood, Anger & Behaviour

Mood swings · difficulty managing anger · ADHD concerns · behavioural changes

Careful assessment and personalised care to help you understand and manage what you're experiencing.
